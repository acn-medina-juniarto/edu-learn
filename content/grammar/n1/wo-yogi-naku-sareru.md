を余儀なくされる says an unwanted outcome was **forced by circumstances**, not chosen. It appears in news, reports and formal explanations to clients, where it signals "this wasn't our preference".

It's written-style and heavy. In conversation, 〜せざるを{得|え}ない or simply 〜しなければならなくなった is more natural.
