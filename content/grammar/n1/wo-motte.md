をもって is a formal marker you'll meet in announcements, letters and speeches. It has two main uses:

- **An ending point**: {本日|ほんじつ}をもちまして{閉店|へいてん}いたします ("We close as of today"). It's very common in notices of service changes or resignations.
- **Means**: {書面|しょめん}をもって ("in writing"), {誠意|せいい}をもって ("with sincerity").

In speech at ceremonies you'll hear をもちまして. In everyday talk, use で instead: {今日|きょう}で{終|お}わります.
