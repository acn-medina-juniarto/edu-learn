〜次第 is a businesslike "as soon as". The second half is **your own future action**, often a promise to the client: ご{連絡|れんらく}いたします, {発送|はっそう}いたします. You can't use it for past events (✗ {届|とど}き{次第|しだい}、{連絡|れんらく}しました).

Careful: Noun + **次第です** means "depends on": {天気|てんき}{次第|しだい}です ("It depends on the weather").
