に基づいて links a decision or action to its **basis**: data, rules, a contract, someone's request. It sounds objective and professional, which is why reports and contracts love it.

The written form **に基づき** (no て) is common in formal documents and emails. Before a noun, use **に基づく**: データに{基|もと}づく{判断|はんだん} ("a data-based decision").
