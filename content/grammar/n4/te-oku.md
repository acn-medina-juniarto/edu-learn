〜ておく adds the idea of **preparation**: you do something now so it's ready later. It's everywhere in office talk: {予約|よやく}しておきます, {確認|かくにん}しておきます.

In speech it often shortens to 〜とく: コピーしとくね. Keep the full form with clients.
