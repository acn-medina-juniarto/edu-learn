〜ことになりました announces a decision **without putting yourself at the center**. It sounds modest and is the natural way to share news at work: a transfer, a new role, a schedule change.

Compare 〜ことにする, which is a decision **you** made: {毎朝|まいあさ}{早|はや}く{来|く}ることにしました ("I've decided to come in early every morning").

The email builder's greeting template uses 担当させていただく**ことになりました**.
