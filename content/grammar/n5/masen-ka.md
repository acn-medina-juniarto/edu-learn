Asking with a negative (〜ませんか, "won't you…?") sounds softer than 〜ましょう ("let's…"), because it leaves room to say no. That's why it's the standard way to invite a colleague.

To decline an invitation politely, avoid a flat いいえ. Try すみません、{今日|きょう}はちょっと…. Trailing off is a common, gentle way to say no.
