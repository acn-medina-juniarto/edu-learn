〜てください is the first polite request you learn. It works well with colleagues, and it's how notices and instructions are written ({資料|しりょう}を{読|よ}んでください).

**In business:** to a client or a senior, 〜てください can sound like an *instruction*. When you're asking a favor, step up to 〜ていただけますか or ご〜いただけますでしょうか. Lesson 4 covers the full ladder.
