ように states a **goal you can't control directly**: a state you're aiming for (not being late, being easy to understand). For an action you control, use ために: {契約|けいやく}を{取|と}るために{訪問|ほうもん}する.

In apologies it's the prevention step: このようなことがない**よう**、{確認|かくにん}を{徹底|てってい}いたします. Written Japanese often drops the に.
